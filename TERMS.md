## What the service is

The connector answers transit questions — nearby stops, upcoming departures,
trip plans, and service alerts — using public timetable and realtime data
aggregated by Transitland from transit agencies worldwide. It is provided
free of charge.

## Acceptable use

- Use the connector for personal, everyday transit information.
- Don't abuse it: no automated scraping, no flooding it with requests, no

## Accuracy

They can be incomplete, delayed, or wrong — agencies don't always publish
realtime data, and schedules change. **Don't rely on the connector for
time-critical travel; always confirm with your transit agency before
heading out.** The connector is not liable for missed buses, trains, or
connections.
